SuperDialer Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026 · Last updated: November 3, 2026

In one line — your call log, contacts and texts never leave your phone. If you create an (optional) account, only your email and your app settings, bookmarks and game scores are stored on a server, and you can delete your account at any time inside the app. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

1. What this app is

SuperDialer is an Android phone app made by an individual (calls, recents, contacts, a built-in web browser and built-in games). It is not on a store; it is distributed as an APK from GitHub. Developer: GitHub Jangwoo0827.

3. Information stored on a server if you create an account (optional)

An account is optional. Every feature works without signing in. If you sign in, the following is stored.

  • Account information: your email address, a unique account ID and how you sign in (email/password or Google). If you sign in with Google, the app receives the email and name (profile) Google provides. Passwords are stored encrypted.
  • Synced data: start-page links, bookmarks, best game scores, display settings (theme, text size, keypad sound, search engine, language-independent options) and the decline message texts.
  • Leaderboard: your nickname and best score per game. These two are visible to other signed-in users. Your email is not. You can change the nickname in Settings.
  • Checklist note (a separate web app that uses the same account): the checklist data you wrote.

Your call log, contacts, texts, call notes, contact notes and recordings are not uploaded to the account.

Information received from Google is used only for signing in and identifying the account, never for advertising or other purposes.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Phone (CALL_PHONE, the default phone app role): placing and answering calls, the in-call screens, blocking numbers
  • Reading/writing the call log, reading/writing contacts, reading/sending texts: the features in 2 above
  • Microphone: only when you press the record button, or when a website asks and you allow it
  • Camera: when you place or answer a video call (to send your picture to the other person; the app does not store the video), and when a website in the built-in browser asks and you allow it
  • Location (precise/approximate): only when a website in the built-in browser asks and you allow it. Websites can ask for the camera, microphone and location only on secure (https) pages, and the app does not collect or store this information
  • Notification access (optional): only when you turn on the KakaoTalk notification inbox above. You must allow it yourself in the system settings and can turn it off at any time. Notifications of apps other than KakaoTalk are not processed
  • Notifications and full-screen notifications: incoming and missed calls, new version alerts
  • Internet: the built-in browser, account sync, update checks
  • Install packages: when you download and install a new version inside the app (if you allowed it)

Every permission is asked for the first time you use the matching feature, and the rest of the app keeps working if you refuse.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Deleting your account: tap Settings > Account > Delete account in the app and the account on the server and the data in 3 above (synced data, leaderboard entries, checklist notes, sign-in info) are erased at once. This can't be undone.
  • If you only sign out, the data on the server stays. To erase it completely, delete the account.
  • Data on the phone is erased by uninstalling the app or via Settings > Apps > SuperDialer > Storage > Clear data. Delete call recording files yourself in a file app.
  • If you can't use the app, ask for account deletion through the contact below.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information and to stop its processing. Most of this you can do in the app (change nickname, delete account, sign out); send any other request to the contact below.

8. Children

Children under 14 may not create an account without a legal guardian's consent. Every feature works without an account. If we learn that an account belongs to a child without consent, we delete it.

9. Security

Communication with the server uses HTTPS, and server data is limited per account (row-level security) so only you can read and write it (except the leaderboard nickname and score). The app opens no channel through which a web page could call app functions, and the built-in games only load files bundled in the app.

10. When this policy changes

When it changes the date on this page is updated, and important changes are noted in the app's update notice.
